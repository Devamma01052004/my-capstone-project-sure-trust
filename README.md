# Pollution in India: Air Quality, Water Contamination, and Policy Impact (2010–2025)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive **end-to-end data analytics case study** focused on analyzing environmental pollution trends across India between **2010 and 2025**. The study integrates multiple analytical tools and techniques to explore air pollution, water contamination, industrial emissions, vehicular emissions, and the potential impact of environmental policies.

The project follows a real-world analytics workflow, beginning with raw data exploration and ending with an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to support decision-making and policy evaluation.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

India faces significant environmental challenges due to rapid urbanization, industrial growth, and increasing vehicular emissions. Understanding the factors contributing to pollution is essential for developing effective intervention strategies.

This project aims to:

* Analyze air and water pollution patterns across India.
* Identify pollution hotspots and contributing factors.
* Evaluate the impact of industrial and vehicular emissions.
* Perform statistical analyses to validate observed trends.
* Simulate environmental improvement scenarios.
* Develop an interactive dashboard for stakeholders and policymakers.
